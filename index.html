Ratings are fit from the uploaded game history using a ratio-based Poisson attack/defense model. NFL scoring is lumpier than soccer or hockey goals (touchdowns, field goals, and extra points come in fixed chunks rather than one point at a time), so treat the exact point total as a rougher approximation than the win probability and over/under — both of which are more robust to that shape mismatch. This is a simplified, browser-only model — indicative only, not betting advice.
